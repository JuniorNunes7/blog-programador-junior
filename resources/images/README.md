## Prompt da arte editorial

> Create a finished abstract editorial paper collage background for the personal Brazilian software blog Programador Junior. Landscape 3:2 composition. Warm ivory paper #F6F1E7 with subtle natural fiber grain, a sweeping dark petroleum green #245C53 torn paper shape in the lower right, a restrained terracotta #B84B2D curved torn strip, a small graphite #252724 fragment. Inspired by independent literary magazines and a notebook of someone still learning. Flat matte handmade cut paper, elegant, restrained, expansive negative space on the left two thirds for real typography to be composited later. No text, no letters, no symbols, no gradients, no shadows, no computers, no faces. The supplied brand panel is composition reference only; do not reproduce it.

## Prompt da adaptação da 404

> Edit this existing humorous 404 illustration for a mobile website. Preserve recognizable helmet, surprised pose with hand on head, armor, head-to-upper-thigh visibility and completely visible swim briefs. Keep the interstellar landscape background and confused punctuation near head. Remove empty space on the right by reframing. No additional text. Do not change costume, character identity or briefs.
